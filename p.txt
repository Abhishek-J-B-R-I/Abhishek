## Create a Premium Cinematic Tech Portfolio Intro Animation

I want a visually stunning, futuristic, high-energy portfolio opening animation for my personal portfolio as a Flutter and mobile application developer. Use the animation reference image as the visual direction. The result must feel like a premium technology brand intro, not a slideshow or a sequence of static images.

### Visual Theme
Use a deep navy background with electric blue, cyan, violet, and purple neon lighting. Create a cinematic atmosphere with glowing particles, energy trails, dynamic light streaks, soft bloom, depth, and subtle 3D movement. The animation should feel alive, immersive, and technically sophisticated.

### Animation Sequence

**1. Logo Reveal — The First Impact**  
Begin with a nearly black navy screen. A tiny blue-purple light appears in the center, emitting subtle sparks and a circular energy pulse. The particles accelerate and spiral inward, gradually constructing my “A” logo from glowing fragments. The logo should emerge with a powerful neon glow, a slight 3D rotation, and a smooth scale-up effect. Add a luminous reflection and a brief energy burst when the logo locks into place.

**2. Colorful Energy Orbit**  
Once the logo appears, create beautiful flowing ribbons of cyan, electric blue, and violet light that orbit around it in elliptical paths. Add hundreds of tiny glowing particles moving along these paths at different speeds and depths. Some particles should leave short light trails, while others sparkle and fade naturally. The energy ribbons should weave in front of and behind the logo to create a convincing sense of depth. Include subtle camera movement and pulsing light synchronized with the motion. This should be the visual highlight of the intro.

**3. Name and Identity Reveal**  
The energy trails converge into a bright flash, then gracefully reveal “ABHISHEK” with bold, modern typography. Beneath it, reveal “Software Engineer | Flutter & Backend-Integrated Application Development.” Use a subtle letter-by-letter glow, a clean motion transition, and a faint luminous underline. Keep the logo visible as the visual anchor.

**4. Floating Technology Ecosystem**  
Transform the orbiting particles into floating technology symbols representing Flutter, Firebase, REST APIs, databases, and mobile development. Each symbol should materialize from particles, illuminate briefly, and move along elegant orbital paths around the logo. Use layered depth, smooth rotation, subtle parallax, and controlled bursts of light. Avoid simple icons sliding horizontally across the screen.

**5. Mobile Application Showcase**  
The energy orbit expands and creates a transition into three elegant floating smartphone mockups. Showcase the Loyalty Rewards App, Real-Time Chat App, and Weather Forecast App. The phones should rotate slightly in 3D space, float at different depths, and receive subtle neon edge lighting. UI elements inside the phones may animate gently to suggest real, working applications. Use smooth camera movement and light trails to connect the showcase to the central brand identity.

**6. Tagline and Loading Sequence**  
The phones move gracefully toward the edges as the energy ribbons return to the center. Reveal the tagline: “Turning Ideas Into Real-World Mobile Applications.” Animate a luminous progress bar that fills smoothly while tiny particles travel along its edge. Keep the background dynamic with subtle moving gradients and slowly drifting particles.

**7. Completion and Portfolio Transition**  
The progress bar completes with a bright but tasteful energy pulse. A glowing circular ring forms around a checkmark, which draws itself with a smooth neon stroke. Display “Welcome to my Portfolio” beneath it. Let the ring expand into a full-screen wave of blue-purple light that seamlessly reveals the actual portfolio homepage.

### Motion and Quality Requirements
- Every element must have its own purposeful animation; do not simply fade or slide complete images onto the screen.
- Prioritize particle systems, animated energy ribbons, glowing paths, 3D rotations, depth, parallax, and fluid transitions.
- Use smooth acceleration and deceleration, organic motion, and cinematic timing rather than repetitive preset animations.
- Keep the logo and name as the primary focal points; visual effects must enhance the brand, not overwhelm it.
- Make the sequence cohesive, with each stage naturally transforming into the next.
- Target approximately 6–8 seconds for the complete intro.
- The final transition must reveal the real portfolio homepage, not a screenshot or a static mockup.
- The experience must feel premium, responsive, and optimized for smooth performance.

**Most importantly:** Do not create a slideshow, carousel, or animation where static images simply enter and exit the screen. Build a continuous, immersive tech experience in which particles, light trails, energy ribbons, and animated elements move organically around the logo. The colorful orbital energy effect from the reference image is the signature visual element and must receive special attention.
